The most important thing first: the **binding intervals for your car** are in the **manufacturer’s service manual**
or shown by the **service indicator** on the dashboard. General figures from the internet do not replace them. This
guide explains how the intervals work, which **TCS and ADAC guideline values** apply – and what you need to know in
Switzerland about warranties and the free choice of garage.

---

## ⏱️ Time or mileage: whichever comes first

A service becomes due **either after a certain mileage or after a certain time**, whichever comes first.

| Example from the service plan | What it means | Recommendation |
|-------------------------------|---------------|----------------|
| “2 years **or** 30,000 kilometres” | Service at the latest after 2 years **or** 30,000 km | Keep it even if you drive little! |
| “1 year **or** 15,000 kilometres” | Service at the latest after 1 year **or** 15,000 km | Ideal for high-mileage drivers |

**⚠️ Why time matters:** wear also occurs **when the car stands still** – for example through the ageing of seals,
fluids and rubber parts ([ADAC](https://presse.adac.de/meldungen/adac-ev/technik/wartungsintervalle-einhalten.html)).

### Fixed versus flexible intervals

| Type of interval | Description | Suitable for | Source |
|------------------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| **Fixed intervals** | Rigid deadlines (e.g. every 15,000 km or 1 year) | Low mileage, gentle driving | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/wartungsintervalle/) |
| **Flexible intervals** | Adjusted to driving style and conditions (e.g. sensors) | High-mileage drivers, varied use | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/wartungsintervalle/) |

**💡 Tip:** if the **service indicator** comes on, book an appointment soon. And even **after the warranty has
expired**, the TCS says the car should go to the garage regularly according to the manufacturer’s specifications
([TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/pannenpraevention.php)).

## 📊 Guideline values for individual jobs

Here are the **recommended intervals** for typical maintenance work – **always prioritise the manufacturer’s
specifications!**

### 🛢️ Fluids

| Job | Interval | Important | Source |
|-----|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Engine oil** | 15,000–30,000 km **or** 1–2 years | **Depends on the maker!** Examples: Opel every 30,000 km or 1 year, Audi flexible within 2 years up to a max. of 30,000 km | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/oelwechsel/) |
| **Brake fluid** | **At the latest every 2 years** | Must be kept! It absorbs water over time → **the boiling point drops**, braking performance suffers | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/bremsfluessigkeit/) |
| **Coolant** | Per the manufacturer’s specification | Protects against overheating and corrosion | — |
| **Gearbox oil** | Per the manufacturer’s specification | Many manufacturers: **no change necessary**. ADAC recommends about 60,000–100,000 km for automatics, about 100,000 km for manuals | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/getriebeoel/) |
| **Power steering fluid** | Per the manufacturer’s specification | Replace immediately if leaking | — |

**⚠️ Brake fluid warning:** at a **water content of 3.5 %** the fluid is at the end of its life
([TCS interview on Blick](https://www.blick.ch/auto/service/tcs-ratgeber-warum-die-bremsfluessigkeit-wechseln-id16867036.html)).

### ⚙️ Mechanical parts

| Part | Interval | Important | Source |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Timing belt** | 60,000–240,000 km **or** 5–10 years | **Must be kept!** If the belt snaps, the engine is often finished | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/ausstattung-technik-zubehoer/zahnriemenwechsel/) |
| **Timing chain** | Usually for life | If you hear noises (clattering), have it checked immediately! | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/ausstattung-technik-zubehoer/steuerkette/) |
| **Auxiliary belt** | 60,000–100,000 km | Drives the ancillaries (alternator, power steering) | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/wartung-inspektion/keilriemen/) |

### 🎯 Filters

| Filter | Interval | Important | Source |
|--------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Air filter** | 15,000–30,000 km **or** 1–2 years | A clogged filter means **loss of power, higher consumption** | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/luftfilter/) |
| **Cabin filter** | **At the latest after 12 months** | Rule of thumb: change it **with the tyres** | [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/klimaanlage-desinfizieren/) |
| **Fuel filter** | Per the manufacturer’s specification | Especially important for diesel | — |
| **Oil filter** | **At every oil change** | — | — |

### 🚗 Tyres

| Criterion | Interval | Recommendation | Source |
|-----------|----------|----------------|--------|
| **Minimum tread depth** | — | **1.6 mm** (legal), TCS recommends **summer: ≥ 3 mm, winter: ≥ 4 mm** | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/reifen/faq-reifen.php) |
| **Tyre age** | — | **Maximum 8 years** – even with enough tread! | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/reifen/faq-reifen.php) |
| **Changing tyres** | Seasonal | **From O to O** (October to Easter) on winter tyres | — |

**💡 Tip:** **check the pressure monthly** – on **cold** tyres!

### ⚡ Battery and wipers

| Part | Interval | Important | Source |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Battery** | Check yearly **from the 4th year** | Usually needs replacing after **3–6 years** | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/tests/autozubehoer/autobatterie-test.php) / [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/batterie/) |
| **Wiper blades** | **Yearly** | Driving on worn wipers risks the TCS charging you with **gross negligence** | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/tests/autozubehoer/scheibenwischer.php) |

## 🚗 Tyre checklist (before every change of season)

- [ ] **Check the tread:** minimum **1.6 mm** (legal), better **≥ 3 mm (summer) / ≥ 4 mm (winter)**
- [ ] **Check the age:** replace tyres older than **8 years**
- [ ] **Check for damage:** cracks, bulges, embedded foreign objects
- [ ] **Check the pressure:** **cold** (at least 3 hours without driving)
- [ ] **Uneven wear?** → you need a **wheel alignment**
- [ ] **Winter tyres:** **from October to Easter** (no legal obligation, but the insurer may reduce a payout after
  an accident)

## 🔧 Can I use an independent garage without losing the warranty?

**✅ Yes!** Since 1 January 2024 the Swiss Federal Council’s **ordinance on motor vehicles** has applied in
Switzerland ([UPSA/AGVS](https://www.agvs-upsa.ch/de/recht-politik/aktuelle-themen/kfz-verordnung/)).

### 📜 Important rules

| Question | Answer | Source |
|----------|--------|--------|
| **Can I use an independent garage?** | **Yes!** The manufacturer’s warranty may not be tied to brand dealerships | [Ordinance, Art. 4](https://www.agvs-upsa.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/AGVS_ROOT/AGVS-UPSA/Recht_und_Politik/Politik/Aktuelle_Themen/KFZ-Verordnung/AS-2023-772-DE.pdf) |
| **Exception?** | Only if the work was carried out **faultily** | [Comco explanations](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/85113.pdf) |
| **What must I observe?** | **Keep the intervals and be able to prove it!** (invoices, stamps) | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/servicekosten.php) |

### ⚠️ Careful with warranty claims

Even with the free choice of garage, these conditions apply:

| Manufacturer | Condition | Source |
|--------------|-----------|--------|
| **AMAG (VW group)** | All service work done **as specified** and **recorded** – otherwise the warranty provider is released | [AMAG warranty terms](https://www.volkswagen.ch/idhub/content/dam/onehub_pkw/importers/ch/dienstleistungen/garantie/AGB%20marken%C3%BCbergreifend%20Garantie%20_D_.pdf) |
| **Other brands** | Similar rules – **when in doubt follow the manufacturer’s specifications** | — |

**💡 TCS recommendation:** have the car serviced **at the brand dealership** in the **early years**, especially if
you want extra services such as the mobility warranty
([TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/servicekosten.php)).

## 🏔️ Swiss peculiarities: the influence of topography

With its mountainous landscape, Switzerland is **not a typical operating environment** for vehicles. Anyone who
drives regularly in the mountains, a lot in **stop-and-go city traffic** or covers **high mileages** subjects their
vehicle to **above-average strain**.

### 🔄 Adjusting the intervals

| Driving profile | Recommended adjustment | Reason |
|-----------------|------------------------|--------|
| **Mountain driving (frequent)** | **Shorter intervals** for oil, brakes, suspension | Higher strain from corners, climbs, braking |
| **City driving (stop-and-go)** | **More frequent oil changes** (every 10,000–15,000 km) | Engine runs longer under load, higher temperatures |
| **High mileage (over 15,000 km/year)** | **Shorter intervals** for oil, tyres, timing belt | Longer running time, higher strain |
| **Normal driving (under 15,000 km/year)** | A **yearly service** usually suffices | Keep the manufacturer’s standard intervals |

**⚠️ Important:** manufacturers’ **standard service plans** are designed for average use. With Switzerland’s
topography and the specific strains, **shorter intervals** are often necessary.

## ✅ Service checklist: what to check after every service

- [ ] **Service book / service plan updated** (stamp or digital entries)
- [ ] **Invoice received and kept** (with date, mileage, work done)
- [ ] **Next service date noted** (in the calendar or in the app)
- [ ] **Dashboard warning lights checked** (e.g. oil level, brakes)
- [ ] **Tyre pressure checked**
- [ ] **Fluid levels checked** (engine oil, brake fluid, coolant, screenwash)

## 📚 When is a service particularly worthwhile?

| Situation | Recommendation | Benefit |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| **Before selling** | Have a **full service done** | Higher selling price (“inspected” or “fully serviced”) |
| **Before a long journey** | **Full inspection** | Avoids breakdowns en route |
| **After winter/summer** | **Tyre change + service** | Optimal condition for the new season |
| **Warning lights on** | **See a garage immediately** | Prevents consequential damage |
| **After an accident** | **Complete check** | Preserves safety and value |
