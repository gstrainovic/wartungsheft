The **service book** (also called the service record) is gone – lost in a move, never handed over by the previous
owner, or left in the glovebox of a car you sold. That is annoying, because when you **sell** and when you claim a
**warranty** the proof of maintenance is what counts. The **good news**: most of the history can usually be
**rebuilt**. **⚠️ Important:** a complete service book can raise the selling price by **5–10 %** – without proof
you are missing that argument
([TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/elektronisches-serviceheft.php)).
Here is how to proceed, step by step.

---

## 🔍 Step 1: is the book already digital?

More than a dozen brands have replaced the printed book with a **digital service book**. The entries then sit with
the manufacturer, and you can request an extract at any time
([TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/elektronisches-serviceheft.php)).

### 📱 Digital service books by brand

| Brand | Digital since | How to access | Cost | Source |
|-------|---------------|---------------|------|--------|
| **Mercedes-Benz** | Mid-2008 | Ask the brand dealership | Usually free | [Mercedes-Benz](https://b2bconnect.mercedes-benz.com/de/help/faq/software/dsb) |
| **VW group (AMAG)** | 2010s | Brand dealership or online portal | Usually free | [AMAG](https://www.amag-import.ch/content/dam/amag-import/technische-informationen/20210101_Allgemeine_Geschaeftsbedingungen_GARANTIE_amag.pdf.coredownload.pdf) |
| **Toyota** | 2015+ | MyToyota app (with Connected Services) | Free | [Toyota Switzerland](https://de.toyota.ch/owners/faq-connected-services) |
| **Mazda** | 2005 | Free app | Free | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/elektronisches-serviceheft.php) |
| **BMW** | 2018+ | ConnectedDrive, BMW app | Free | [BMW Switzerland](https://www.bmw.ch/de/owners/connecteddrive/connecteddrive-services.html) |
| **Audi** | 2017+ | myAudi portal | Free | [Audi Switzerland](https://www.audi.ch/ch/de/owners/connected-services/audi-connect.html) |

**💡 Tip for independent garages:** in Switzerland, **independent garages** can look up the digital service books of
many brands via the **TecRMI Service Book** from Hostettler Autotechnik. The system covers **23 brands**; one
lookup costs the garage **CHF 5.50 plus VAT**
([Autotechnik](https://www.autotechnik.ch/de/dienstleistungen/digitales-serviceheft/)).

## 📋 Step 2: order a duplicate from the dealership

If your brand still keeps a **printed book**, order an **official duplicate** from the **brand dealership**.

### 💰 Cost and process

| Step | What to do | Cost | Time | Source |
|------|------------|------|------|--------|
| 1. **Contact the garage** | Ask the brand dealership for a duplicate | **about CHF 30** (according to SRF Espresso) | 1–2 weeks | [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/umwelt-und-verkehr-verlorenes-auto-serviceheft-aufwand-fuer-duplikat-lohnt-sich) |
| 2. **Have entries added** | Visit every garage that has serviced the car | Usually free | 1–2 days per garage | [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/umwelt-und-verkehr-verlorenes-auto-serviceheft-aufwand-fuer-duplikat-lohnt-sich) |

**⚠️ Important:** a **simple copy of the old book has no value** – it has to be an **official duplicate** from the
brand!

---

## 🧾 Step 3: gather the invoices

Every **garage invoice** states the date, the work done and usually the **mileage**. That makes it proof of a
service – **even without a stamp**.

### 📍 Where to find invoices

Before you call anyone, **go through your paperwork**. You need everything that proves **date, mileage and garage**:

| Source | What to do | Chance of success |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| **Your own documents** | Search your papers, emails, cloud storage | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Garages** | Contact every garage that has serviced the car | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Previous owner** | If bought used: contact the seller | ⭐⭐ |
| **Digital archives** | Check the brands’ customer portals | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Bank statements** | Search for garage names of the last 5 years | ⭐⭐ |
| **MFK inspection reports** | From the road traffic office: mileage at the inspection date | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Tyre change receipts** | Proves regular maintenance | ⭐⭐ |

**💡 Tip:** **sort everything chronologically** in one folder (digital or paper). Per entry: **date, mileage,
garage, work done**.

**💡 Tip:** many garages keep invoices for **10 years or longer** in their workshop software and can print copies
or send them by email.

---

## 📄 Step 4: obtain the MFK inspection reports

The **MFK inspection reports** record the **odometer reading**. Together with the invoices they create a **complete
mileage history**.

### 📋 How to get inspection reports

| Source | What to do | Cost | Time | Source |
|--------|------------|------|------|--------|
| **Your own documents** | Search your papers | Free | Immediately | — |
| **Road traffic office** | Written request | **Charged** | 1–2 weeks | [Canton Lucerne](https://strassenverkehrsamt.lu.ch/strassenverkehr/halterauskunft_sperre) |
| **TCS (if inspected there)** | Ask at the TCS section | Usually free for members | 1–2 days | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/kurse-fahrzeugchecks/fahrzeugkontrollen/motorfahrzeugkontrolle-mfk.php) |

**💡 Important:** information **beyond the vehicle registration document** generally has to be requested **in
writing** from the office and is charged for.

## 🚗 Step 5: when nothing can be found

If the history **cannot be rebuilt**, what remains is an **independent proof of the vehicle’s condition**. The four
steps above (sorting paperwork, contacting garages, recreating the book) have already taken you this far; now it is
about finding a solid substitute.

### 📌 Swiss peculiarities

**⚠️ Important:** in Switzerland there is **no central office** that issues a new service book:
- **Road traffic office:** keeps **no maintenance history**
- **Manufacturer:** only stores the **official brand workshop entries** (if any)
- **Independent garages:** keep their records **locally in their software** (usually 5–10 years)

**💡 Tip:** every Swiss garage keeps a **customer file** – with your **chassis number** or **VIN** you can request a
**copy of the service history**. At **brand workshops** (BMW ASE, VW digital service book, etc.) this is often
**immediate** as a printout; at independent garages allow **1–3 days**.

### 🔍 Options for proof

| Method | What is checked? | Cost | Time | Usefulness for selling | Source |
|--------|------------------|------|------|------------------------|--------|
| **TCS used-car test** | Around 90 minutes of checks, full report | **CHF 150–200** | 1–2 days | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | [TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/kurse-fahrzeugchecks/fahrzeugkontrollen/occasion-auto-testen.php) |
| **DEKRA/GTÜ (imported vehicles)** | Full check with report | **CHF 200–300** | 1 day | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | — |
| **Garage check** | Basic check with short report | **CHF 80–150** | 1 day | ⭐⭐⭐ | — |

**⚠️ Important:** a **check does not replace the service book**, but it gives a buyer **something solid** to hold
on to.

## ✅ Checklist: rebuilding the history

### 📌 Phase 1: immediate measures
- [ ] **Check digital books** (brand app, online portal, TecRMI via the garage)
- [ ] **Search all your documents** (folder, emails, cloud)
- [ ] **Contact the brand dealership for a duplicate** (cost: about CHF 30)

### 📌 Phase 2: gather the invoices
- [ ] **List every garage** that has serviced the car
- [ ] **Call each garage** and ask for invoice copies
- [ ] **Contact the previous owner** (if bought used)

### 📌 Phase 3: official documents
- [ ] **Obtain the MFK inspection reports** for all available years
- [ ] **Check the vehicle registration document** for entries
- [ ] **Check insurance policies** for maintenance notes

### 📌 Phase 4: fallbacks
- [ ] **Have a TCS used-car test done** (CHF 150–200)
- [ ] **Look at alternatives** (DEKRA, GTÜ, garage check)

## 💡 Tips for selling without a service book

Even without a complete service book you can raise your car’s value:

| Measure | Effect | Cost |
|---------|--------|------|
| **TCS used-car test** | Proves roadworthy condition | CHF 150–200 |
| **Fresh service** | “Fully serviced” / “inspected” raises the price | CHF 50–200 |
| **Present the invoices** | Shows you cared for the car | — |
| **Show inspection reports** | Proves the mileage | — |
| **Offer a warranty** | Personal commitment (e.g. “3 months warranty”) | — |

**💡 TCS tip:** a **complete service book** can raise the selling price by **5–10 %**
([TCS](https://www.tcs.ch/de/testberichte-ratgeber/ratgeber/kontrollen-unterhalt/elektronisches-serviceheft.php)).

## ⚠️ Important: no gaps from now on!

So that you **never lose** your service book again:

- [ ] **Scan or photograph every invoice** immediately
- [ ] Set up a **digital backup** in the cloud (Google Drive, Dropbox)
- [ ] Keep the **service book in the same place** (e.g. the glovebox)
- [ ] After every service, **check the entry is there**
- [ ] **Note the mileage after every service** (useful for later price negotiations)
